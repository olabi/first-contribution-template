# Optional peer review and behavioral rubric

Manual extension, not an implemented scoring feature. Agree on purpose, version, timing, role-sensitive criteria, weights, minimum other-person raters, access, retention, and appeal handling before work. Self-reflection is separate from ratings by others.

| Criterion / example weight | 1: substantial support needed | 2: partly met | 3: agreed level met | 4: evidenced additional support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agreed role / 40 | Progress remains unclear after support and renegotiation | Partial delivery; changes not fully communicated | Delivers scope or renegotiates changes in time | Meets own role and provides agreed support that advances team work |
| Verification / 25 | Required checks and limitations missing | Some checks; insufficient reproducible evidence | Records agreed checks, results, and limits | Additional reproducible checking finds and helps resolve errors |
| Feedback / 20 | Requested review not completed or renegotiated | Feedback lacks evidence or actionable changes | Gives relevant evidence and actionable suggestions | Follows up to confirm improvement |
| Coordination / 15 | Needed blocker or handoff communication missing | Updates lack timing or context | Communicates blockers, changes, and handoffs in time | Helps resolve dependencies or disagreements and reconfirm decisions |

Use N/A when there was no observation opportunity; do not replace it with a low score. Dissent, quietness, AI use, commit counts, and message volume are not rating criteria. Adjust the rubric for actual project roles before starting.

Record period, rubric version, rater and subject pseudonyms, criterion, 1–4 or N/A, evidence link, observation, improvement suggestion, and conflicts of interest. Do not invent responses. Pseudonyms do not guarantee anonymity in small teams.

## Suggested manual calculation

Require a pre-agreed minimum number of other-person ratings per criterion (for example two). If any required criterion lacks sufficient observations or has an unresolved appeal, hold the overall score. Review large discrepancies with evidence rather than automatically deleting ratings.

For complete ratings, average valid other-person ratings by criterion. Optional 0–100 peer score = sum of criterion weight × (criterion mean - 1) / 3, where weights total 100. Show rating counts and evidence alongside the result.

Accepted-credit points measure contribution quantity against a team plan; peer ratings describe observed performance quality. Report separately by default. Do not directly combine an individual's existing credit points or team-credit percentage with a 0–100 peer score. If a team requires a composite, first define a comparable individual-role completion denominator and both 0–100 scales, then agree a blend (for example 80/20) before work. This is a design option, not current calculator behavior.

## Appeals and communication

Allow each member to review their result and flag missing evidence or errors. Preserve original and corrected records with reasons; hold disputed outcomes. Collect individual ratings through an agreed restricted channel, not public issues or unrestricted Linear comments. Linear is for task questions, feedback, and agreement changes; GitHub stores reviewed versioned agreements. AI may summarize supplied evidence but never decide grades or authorship or invent human approval.
